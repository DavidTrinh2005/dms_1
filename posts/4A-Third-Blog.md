# Wireframing in Figma

---

## Homework

In this lesson, we were introduced to __wireframing__, we were shown different examples of this in class and tasked to create a wireframe for our web. I wanted my web into four stages; the front page (intro), about me section, portofolio section, and the individual assignments. The first page will have a menu control panel and an intro graphic. Scrolling further down will reveal the about me and beyond that is the portfolio section. The about me section will reveal a portrait of myself and beside that is the artist statement. Below that is the portfolio where it will display all three works for people to have a sample thumbnail of each nail. To top it off, I will have a display of every assignment and an explanation of each project and its processes.
